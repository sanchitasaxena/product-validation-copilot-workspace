# Research Plan Prompt

Use this prompt to create or review research plans.

## Requirements

- Start with the decision the research informs.
- Resolve target audience context or enter discovery mode.
- Map each research question to a decision.
- Identify source docs and assumptions.
- Avoid research for its own sake.
- Mark out-of-scope items clearly.

## Output

- Research goal
- Decisions informed
- Audience grounding
- Research questions
- Method recommendation
- Timeline
- Participant criteria
- Source references
- Risks and assumptions
