# Synthesis Prompt

Use this prompt to synthesize research sessions, Slack sprints, survey open text, or QA evidence.

## Evidence rules

- Cite source files.
- Preserve participant quotes verbatim.
- Do not speculate beyond the data.
- Show percentages only when the denominator is clear.
- Separate findings from hypotheses.
- Identify conflicting opinions.
- Connect recommendations to evidence.

## Output

- Summary
- Core findings
- Supporting evidence
- Conflicting signals
- Recommendation
- Next questions
- Source list
