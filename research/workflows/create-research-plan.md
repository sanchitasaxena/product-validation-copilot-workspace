# Create Research Plan Workflow

## Steps

1. Identify the product or roadmap decision the research informs.
2. Resolve target audience context or enter discovery mode.
3. Review source docs.
4. Draft research questions mapped to decisions.
5. Recommend method and timing.
6. Mark out-of-scope items.
7. Ask for human review before generating final DOCX.

## Output

A research plan grounded in source documents and explicit audience context.
