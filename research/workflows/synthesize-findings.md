# Synthesize Findings Workflow

## Steps

1. Read all source transcripts, notes, survey responses, or QA evidence.
2. Preserve participant quotes verbatim.
3. Calculate percentages only when the denominator is clear.
4. Separate findings, hypotheses, recommendations, and conflicting signals.
5. Include source references.
6. If discovery mode was used, propose candidate persona/JTBD/CUJ updates for human review.
